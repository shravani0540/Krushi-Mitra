Agricultural Expert Consultation

Get professional guidance for healthier crops and better yields.

Connect with agricultural experts to receive personalized advice on crop selection, pest management, soil health, irrigation practices, fertilizers, and modern farming techniques.

Why Consult an Expert?

  • ✔ Get crop-specific recommendations.
  • ✔ Identify pests and diseases early.
  • ✔ Improve crop productivity.
  • ✔ Learn modern farming practices.
  • ✔ Reduce unnecessary farming costs.

Expert Advice

  • Choose the right crop for your soil.
  • Use water efficiently.
  • Perform regular soil testing.
  • Apply fertilizers in recommended quantities.
  • Monitor crops regularly for pests.

Important Note

Expert guidance is provided based on the information submitted by farmers. For serious crop diseases or emergencies, farmers should also contact their nearest Agriculture Department or Krishi Vigyan Kendra (KVK).

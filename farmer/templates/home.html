शेतकऱ्यांसाठी एक विश्वासू साथी.(About Krushimitra)

Krushimitra is a digital platform for farmers, bringing market prices, weather updates, crop information, government schemes, and expert advice together in one place — your trusted companion for modern farming.

आमच्या सेवा (Our services)

खालीलपैकी योग्य पर्याय निवडून तुम्हाला हवी ती माहिती लगेच मिळवा. (Select the right option below to get the information you need instantly.)

(बातम्या)

Latest agriculture-related news — government announcements, weather alerts, mandi (market) updates, and farming-related events relevant to farmers.

(बाजारभाव)

Daily/weekly mandi rates for different crops (onion, cotton, soybean, wheat, etc.) across nearby markets, so farmers can decide where and when to sell.

(पिके)

Information on different crops — sowing season, ideal soil, fertilizer needs, common diseases/pests, and care tips for each crop type.

(योजना)

Government schemes and subsidies for farmers — eligibility, benefits, how to apply, and application deadlines (e.g. PM-Kisan, crop insurance, loan waivers).

(तज्ञ)

A way to connect with agricultural experts or advisors — could be a contact form, chat, or call booking for farmers who need personalized advice.

(प्रशिक्षण)

Training videos, guides, or workshop schedules that teach modern farming techniques, equipment use, and best practices.
